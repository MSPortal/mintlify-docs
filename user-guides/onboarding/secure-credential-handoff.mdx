Credential handoff has separate Company and MSP workflows:

- [Company: Hand over credentials](/user-guides/client-onboarding/hand-over-credentials) — submit details in Single or Bulk mode, confirm the handoff, and request a correction.
- [MSP: Retrieve credentials and secure files](/user-guides/client-onboarding/retrieve-secure-submissions) — review the retrieval confirmation, retain the returned information, and handle processed or expired submissions.
- [Company: Upload documents and secure files](/user-guides/client-onboarding/upload-files) — use the correct upload request and check its status.

Use these task guides for current controls, access requirements, and FAQs.
