**Audience:** Company learners. MSP staff can also browse the library and use **Self Enroll** for their own learning. Enrolling other people is an MSP administration task.

Your MSP must enable training, give you access, and make courses available to your company. Premium content can require an additional entitlement. The catalog and available actions can differ between users.

For **Huntress** tiles, follow [Open your Huntress training](/user-guides/training/open-huntress-training). The enrollment steps below apply to portal course cards. Huntress tiles represent existing provider assignments and do not offer these enrollment actions.

## Browse the course library

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the library">
    Select **Training** in the sidebar, then **Training Menu > Course Library** (`/training/library`).

    If Training opens a provider's learning portal instead, use that portal's catalog and assignments. Your MSP has configured a different training experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find a course">
    Search by course title, description, or category. Select **Filters** to narrow the catalog by the categories, tags, course types, and difficulty levels available to you.

    Use the small grid, large grid, or list controls to change the layout. **My Courses** limits the catalog to your enrollments; **Show All** removes that enrollment filter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the course">
    Check its description and any **Required**, **Included**, or **Premium** labels. A **Preview** button, when present, opens a preview. Watching a preview does not enroll you or complete the course.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enroll yourself">
    Select **Enroll** on the course card, or **Enroll Now** in list view. Wait for the enrollment confirmation. If the button is disabled or enrollment fails, check your selected company and ask your MSP to confirm course access.

    For MSP staff, **Self Enroll** enrolls the signed-in staff member. **Enroll Users** is a separate administrative action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start learning">
    Select **Start**, or **Continue** for a course already in progress. See [Take a course and track progress](/user-guides/training/take-a-course).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Find courses you already completed

Completed courses are hidden from the library by default. Open **Filters** and enable **Show completed courses** under **Visibility**. Select **Apply Filters** to return to the catalog. Clear other active filters if the course is still missing.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/product/training-library-filters.png" alt="Visibility section with the Show completed courses checkbox." width="702" height="194" />
</Frame>

For a completed course with a certificate, select **View Certificate**. You can also review enrollment status in **Training Menu > My Training**.

## Remove an enrollment

Where a course shows **Unenroll**, selecting it requests removal of your enrollment. This changes your training record; it is not a way to hide the card temporarily. Use filters to change what you see instead.

Required or assigned training, courses already started, and completed training may be protected from removal. Ask your MSP if an enrollment was assigned incorrectly or removal is refused. Do not unenroll to troubleshoot a missing completion or certificate.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why is the course library empty?">
    Clear the search and filter chips, switch **My Courses** back to **Show All**, and check the selected company. Your MSP may still need to enable courses or your training access. A load-error message should be reported to the MSP rather than treated as an empty catalog.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did my completed course go?">
    The library hides completed courses by default. In **Filters > Visibility**, enable **Show completed courses**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I enroll a coworker?">
    Company learners enroll themselves in available courses. Ask your MSP to assign training to another user or group.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Preview count as completing training?">
    For portal courses, a preview does not complete training: enroll and use **Start** or **Continue**, then complete the required content and assessment. Huntress tiles currently open a preview link even when labeled Start or Continue; use your assigned Huntress training link for tracked completion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can another user see a course that I cannot?">
    Course availability depends on company configuration, permissions, assignments, and premium-training access. Ask your MSP to check the specific course for your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Enroll immediately start the course?">
    Enrollment adds the course to your training. Use **Start** to open its player after enrollment succeeds. Starting an assigned learning-path course may enroll and open it in one action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I remove required or completed training?">
    Training history and assignments can be protected. Ask your MSP to correct the assignment; removing a library filter does not remove an enrollment.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
