**Audience:** MSP staff and Company users granted both Training read and Security Awareness read access. Your MSP must enable Huntress SAT assignment synchronization and map its accounts to the companies you can access. For your own episodes, use [your personal Huntress training](/user-guides/training/open-huntress-training).

## Find an assignment

1. Open **Training > Training Menu > Security Training**.
2. Select **Assignments** when that tab is available. **Training Status** is the separate [learner status reader](/user-guides/training/read-awareness-status).
3. Confirm the company selection and wait for the assignment list to load.
4. Search using **Search assignments, episodes, or companies...**. Assignment descriptions are also searched.
5. Choose **All statuses**, **Draft**, **Staged**, **Launching**, **In progress**, **Completed**, or **Canceled** in the status filter.
6. Use **Previous** and **Next** to browse the 25-row pages. Select **View curriculum** on the required assignment to [read its episodes](/user-guides/training/read-huntress-curriculum).

Search and status changes return to page 1. Changing the main company selection resets search, status, pagination, and any open curriculum panel. The list is ordered by start date, newest first, then name; it has no column-sort or date-range filter.

## Understand the summary and rows

| Summary | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Assignments | Total synced assignments in the selected company scope. |
| Active assignments | Assignments with **Launching** or **In progress** status. Staged is not counted as active here. |
| Episodes | Distinct episode records linked to the scoped assignments. |
| Learner assignments | Sum of assigned learners across assignments. One person assigned to several curricula contributes more than once. |
| Completion | Completed learner-assignment records divided by assigned learner-assignment records; 0% when there are no assigned records. |

The summary describes the selected company scope. Search and status filters narrow the table and its result count, but do not recalculate the summary. Do not interpret a 0% summary without checking whether there are any assigned learners.

Each row shows **Assignment**, **Company**, **Status**, **Episodes**, **Learner progress**, and **Schedule**. Progress is an aggregate completed/assigned count and percentage. It uses synced learner activity indicating completion or at least 100% progress; it is not a list of individual learner names or independent proof that every episode was passed.

## Missing access or results

If Assignments is absent or the direct page is unavailable, ask your MSP to check both permissions and Huntress SAT assignment-feed configuration. General Huntress security access does not guarantee this tab. Some tenants also use a different Training experience.

For **No assignments match these filters**, clear search and choose All statuses. For **No Huntress assignments yet**, your MSP should check company mappings and the assignment sync. For **Huntress training could not be loaded**, reload later or report the error; the page has no dedicated retry button. Summary figures can remain visible during a failed or pending filtered request, so they do not prove that request succeeded.

Existing synced content can remain available while an integration is paused. Treat it as historical data until your MSP confirms freshness. This page does not show a last-sync control, run a provider sync, create or edit assignments, enroll learners, send reminders, cancel a curriculum, or export CSV.

Company users should request changes through the [Home AI Assistant](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket), then review and confirm the request. MSP staff use the provider's supported administration workflow and can consult the [Huntress integration guide](/user-guides/integrations/enable-huntress-integration).

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why do the summary counts stay the same after filtering?">
    They describe the full selected company scope. Search and status filter the table and its result count only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Learner assignments count unique people?">
    No. The same person contributes once for each assignment they are assigned to.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I create, cancel, or send a Huntress assignment here?">
    No. This page reads synced assignments. Your MSP manages provider administration separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does View curriculum show exactly which learners finished?">
    No. It shows aggregate learner progress and the episode list. Use the learner status view or ask your MSP to inspect individual provider records.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
