## Find the right training page

Use **Training Menu** at the top of a training page. The menu and pages depend on your access and configured integrations.

| Page | Purpose | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Course Library | Browse available courses and start your enrollments | Training access and course availability |
| My Training | Review your own enrollments and progress | Training access |
| All Enrollments | Manage enrollments across permitted users and companies | MSP training-management access |
| Learning Paths | Manage paths, assignments, groups, and templates | MSP training-management access |
| Reports & Analytics | Review training completion and reporting | MSP training-management access |
| Security Training | Review connected security-awareness training | Security-awareness access and a supported configured integration |

The **Training** landing page can show assigned learning paths or the course library. If your MSP uses direct provider-hosted training, Training can open that learning experience instead.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can every portal user access all training pages?">
    No. Your MSP controls training permissions, company scope, course availability, and premium access. Administrative menu items are separate from learner access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who assigns training or changes a due date?">
    Your MSP manages assignments and due dates. Company users complete the training made available to them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my Training page look different from a colleague's?">
    Assignments, available courses, role permissions, and provider configuration can differ. Ask your MSP to check the particular course or feature you need.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where should I report a training problem?">
    Contact your MSP with the course title, the page you were using, and the exact error. Include whether the problem concerns access, enrollment, playback, progress, or a certificate.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
