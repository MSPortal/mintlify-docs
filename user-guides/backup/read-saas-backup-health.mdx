**Audience:** Company users and MSP staff with Backup read access, an enabled provider feature, an active connection, and mapped company data.

## Open the report

1. Open **Backup > Microsoft 365**.
2. Select **SaaS Protection** for Kaseya or **Spanning Backup** when several providers are available. With only one available provider, its report opens directly.
3. Confirm the company selection and customers in scope.
4. Expand **Overall backup health** to review **Workload health**.
5. Expand **Backup result trend** and review **Customer backup health** below it.

These reports summarize Exchange, OneDrive, SharePoint, and Teams services. They are not individual-file restore browsers.

## Read coverage and status

**Backed up in the last 24 hours** compares backed-up services with active services in the imported snapshot. The displayed percentage weights the selected customers by their active-service counts; it is not an equal average of company percentages.

Kaseya also shows **Services in initial backup**. For its workload summaries:

| Label | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| **Healthy** | Backed-up services cover the active-service count, with no services still in initial backup. |
| **Needs attention** | Coverage is at least 90% but below full protection, or initial backups remain. |
| **Critical** | Coverage is below 90%. |
| **No data** / **N/A** | There is no usable active-service basis for the calculation. |

Spanning uses its reported workload health status and does not show Kaseya's initial-backup metric. Do not apply Kaseya's percentage thresholds to Spanning's labels.

Overall health summarizes customer statuses. Check individual customer and workload rows even when the overall label is Healthy: a missing workload can still have no data. A percentage from an old snapshot does not establish current protection.

**Last 100% successful backup** summarizes available customer dates. It is not proof that every customer completed a backup at the same moment, and customers without a date require separate investigation. **Last synced** describes imported-data freshness, not when a backup operation ran; the header timestamp should not be assumed to prove every customer has refreshed.

## Review trends and customer rows

Kaseya offers **10 days** and **30 days** for the trend. Spanning uses **7 days**. Expand the trend and hover a plotted point to read its available percentage. The lines combine successful-service coverage by workload and day. Missing dates or a line drawn between points do not prove a backup ran during the gap.

The customer table shows the portal company and mapped provider customer, health, workload ratios, and Last full success. Kaseya also shows Initial. On a narrow screen, scroll the table horizontally to reach its remaining columns. Customer rows do not open a restore or editing form.

## Refresh and investigate missing data

Select **Refresh** to reload the imported report. It does not trigger a provider sync, run a backup, or repair a failed service. Check the displayed dates after refreshing.

If the report says **Backup health could not be loaded**, try Refresh. If the issue continues, Company users should contact their MSP. MSP staff should inspect the selected provider's connection, sync status, company mapping, and feature access. Some shared Spanning empty/error text still mentions Kaseya; use the selected provider to identify the integration to check.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Refresh start a new backup?">
    No. It reloads data already imported into MSPortal. Provider synchronization and backup execution are separate operations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can 100% still need attention in Kaseya?">
    Services can still be in initial backup. Read the initial-backup count and individual workload status alongside the percentage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is Spanning missing the 30-day selector and Initial column?">
    Spanning uses a seven-day trend and its own reported health status. The initial-backup metric and 10/30-day choices belong to Kaseya.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Healthy prove that every workload has data?">
    No. Check individual workload results, missing values, and snapshot freshness. An overall status can coexist with missing workload data.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
