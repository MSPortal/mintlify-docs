The client onboarding portal is the workspace your IT provider shares for a specific onboarding. Company contacts use it to complete assigned work, supply information, review requests, and follow the provider's progress.

<Note>
Company contacts follow the task guides below. MSP staff can use the same guides to explain the client experience; creating templates, configuring access, and managing submissions are separate MSP workflows.
</Note>

## What appears in the workspace

Your provider chooses the visible phases, tasks, documents, approvals, and reference pages. Access can also depend on the role assigned to your onboarding contact. Another contact may see a different set of items.

- **Your tasks** contain work assigned to the client. Open an item to read its instructions and use its specific controls.
- **Provider progress** shows the MSP-owned work your provider makes visible.
- **Documents** include upload requests and reference material. Submitting a file and receiving approval are separate events.
- **Approvals** request a decision from the client. Already decided items show their result.
- **Pages** contain read-only reference articles. Reading a page does not sign off a task.

Depending on the workspace layout, an item opens in a dialog or expands in place. A blocked item identifies prerequisites that must be resolved before you can act on it.

## Know what saves

| Action | Save behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Change a checklist checkbox | Saves that checkbox immediately; overall task completion is separate |
| Type an information response | Submit with **Mark Complete** |
| Fill an onboarding form | Submit with **Save Form**, including edits to a saved entry |
| Answer a linked survey | Submit with **Submit Form** |
| Select files for a regular document request | Starts uploading immediately |
| Choose a secure file | Review and confirm **Encrypt & Upload** |
| Enter credentials or choose a Bulk attachment | Select **Submit Credentials**, then confirm **Submit** |
| Draw a signature | Submit with **I agree and sign** |
| Add approval notes | Included when you select **Approve** or **Reject** |
| Read a page, open a video, or download a calendar file | Does not by itself submit task completion |

Wait for the result on the item before leaving. If the result is uncertain, check its status before repeating the action.

## Questions and progress

Use task comments for discussion about a specific item, or **Ask a Question** for a general onboarding question. Sending a comment does not submit a form or complete its task. Your provider's internal notes are not part of the Company discussion view.

Completing all work visible to you does not necessarily finish the entire onboarding. Other contacts, provider tasks, reviews, and dependencies may still be outstanding. Completed workspaces are read-only.

## MSP preview

MSP staff can use the portal's **View as** preview to inspect a contact's or role's experience when they have the required onboarding permissions. A preview simulates client submissions locally and does not save them as real client responses. Use it to review presentation; an actual invitation or Company access check is still needed to verify that client's access.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a Company administrator manage onboarding users and permissions?">
    The MSP manages Company users and access. Onboarding contact access is also configured by the provider. Ask them to add the right contact or adjust access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can everyone at my company use my invitation?">
    Each contact needs their own access. Forwarding an invitation does not change the contact assigned to it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does another contact see different tasks?">
    Your provider controls visibility and contact-role assignments. Ask them to review the missing item and your contact's access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I finish the provider's work?">
    The client task list and provider progress serve different roles. Complete the items assigned to you; the MSP manages provider-owned work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a successful MSP preview submit a client response?">
    No. Preview interactions are simulations. They do not prove that an invited Company contact has accessed the portal or submitted the task.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I still read a completed portal but cannot save?">
    A completed workspace can remain available for reading while submissions are disabled. Contact your provider if further action is needed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
