Open **Cloud > Licenses** to review the license records synchronized for your company. The inventory supports **Microsoft 365**, **Google Workspace**, and **Adobe**; the rows you see depend on connected services and your access.

**Audience:** Company users with access to the relevant cloud data and MSP staff. Company users should ask their MSP to change access, assignments, or subscriptions.

## Open your license inventory

1. Open **Cloud**, then select the **Licenses** header tab.
2. If you can choose companies, select the company you want to review in the top bar.
3. Keep the **Licenses** view selected below the summary. Use the **License** column filter to find a name or SKU code.
4. Review **Assigned**, **Purchased**, **Available**, and **Renews / Expires**. Check **Last synced** through **Columns** when freshness matters.

**Hide free & trial** and **Hide large allocations** start enabled. A missing row may be hidden by these switches. See [Find, filter, and export licenses](/user-guides/cloud/filter-licenses).

## Understand each provider

| Provider | What the inventory reports | Assigned-user panel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Microsoft 365** | Synchronized assigned and purchased seats, available seats, and utilization. Renewal and trial information appear when subscription data is available. | Click a row to review synchronized users. |
| **Google Workspace** | Distinct assigned users for each product and SKU. Purchased capacity is not supplied by this assignment dataset. | Click a row to review synchronized users. |
| **Adobe** | Synchronized subscription usage, purchased quantity, available quantity, status, and renewal date. | Adobe rows do not open an assigned-user panel. |

Google's capacity columns show **Unavailable**, not zero. In its detail panel those counts use a dash. Verify purchased Google seats with your MSP or provider subscription records.

<Note>
These are synchronized records, not a live billing statement. A license assignment does not prove recent use of the application or establish the price being charged.
</Note>

## Read the table and summary

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **License** | Friendly name and SKU code. **Show SKU** places the code first. |
| **Provider / Company** | Service and company associated with the row. The Provider column is omitted when only one provider is available; Company is omitted when scoped to one company. |
| **Product** | Product family reported for the license. |
| **Assigned** | Recorded assignments or subscription usage. A person with several licenses contributes to several rows. |
| **Purchased** | Known purchased capacity. |
| **Available** | Known purchased seats minus assigned seats, with a minimum of zero. |
| **Utilization** | Rounded assigned-to-purchased percentage, capped at 100%. It needs a positive known purchased count. |
| **Renews / Expires** | Recorded renewal date, when available. Microsoft rows use the earliest available renewal date for that SKU and may show a **Trial** badge. |
| **Status** | Provider-reported status; available through **Columns**. |
| **Last synced** | When this license record was synchronized; available through **Columns**. |

The summary counts matching license records across list pages, not unique people. **Licenses** can include the same SKU at several companies. **Assigned** includes Google assignments, while **Purchased** and **Available** exclude licenses with unknown capacity.

<Tip>
For a capacity review, select one company and one provider. Use the individual row counts. A combined Microsoft/Google summary percentage can be misleading because its assignment total includes Google while its purchased total does not. A 100% bar also cannot show whether assignments exceed purchased capacity; compare the numbers.
</Tip>

The summary disappears when you apply a **License**, **Product**, or **Status** column filter. Clear those filters to see it again. Company, provider, and the three inventory switches update the summary; changing display order with **Show SKU** does not change totals.

## Access and next actions

Your MSP controls your role and company access. Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace license rows require **Read Microsoft 365** access. Adobe rows require **Read Storefront** access. A user with only Adobe access can see the license inventory without the other Cloud tabs or assignment breakdowns.

This page does not assign, remove, purchase, or cancel licenses. Company users can [open a support ticket](/user-guides/ticketing/open-a-ticket) to request a change, or use the [Storefront](/user-guides/storefront/index) where their MSP has enabled the appropriate purchase workflow.

MSP staff can review connected services in **Settings > Integrations**. Check the relevant company mapping and synchronization status before interpreting missing or stale data. This inventory has no **Sync now** button, and simply switching away and back may reuse cached results.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Cloud Licenses support Adobe as well as Microsoft and Google?">
    Yes. Adobe subscription rows appear when Adobe data is synchronized and your role has Storefront read access. Adobe does not provide the assigned-user drill-down used by Microsoft and Google here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Google show Unavailable for Purchased and Available?">
    The Google assignment dataset reports users holding licenses but does not provide purchased seat capacity. Unavailable is not zero. Ask your MSP to check the subscription records for purchased quantities.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Assigned tell me how many people use the software regularly?">
    No. It reports synchronized assignments or usage quantities, not application sign-in activity. One person with several licenses is counted on each applicable license row.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does 100% utilization mean I have exactly enough seats?">
    Not necessarily. The displayed row percentage is capped at 100%, and the combined summary can include assignments whose capacity is unknown. Select one company and provider, compare Assigned with Purchased, and confirm with your MSP before making a purchase decision.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can I see Adobe licenses but not Cloud Users or By domain?">
    Adobe inventory access uses the Storefront read permission. The other Cloud tabs and Microsoft/Google assignment breakdowns require Microsoft 365 read access. Ask your MSP if you need additional access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the renewal date blank, or different from another subscription?">
    A date appears only when synchronized subscription data supplies it. A Microsoft SKU can combine subscriptions and show the earliest available renewal date; Google assignment rows do not supply renewal dates here. Check the subscription record before arranging a renewal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I remove a license or change its quantity here?">
    No. Cloud Licenses is an inventory and review page. Request the change through your MSP, or use an enabled Storefront workflow. Selecting a row or changing a filter does not change the subscription.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why do the summary cards disappear after searching for a license?">
    The summary is hidden when a License, Product, or Status column filter is active. Clear those filters to restore it; the table remains filtered independently.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
