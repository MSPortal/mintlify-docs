Open **Cloud** to review synchronized information from the services connected by your MSP. Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace share the main Cloud views. **Licenses** also supports Adobe subscription inventory, and **Domains** includes company-mapped DNSimple registrar records.

**Audience:** Company users with access to cloud information and MSP staff. Your role, company access, connected providers, and available data determine what you see.

Opening Cloud normally starts at **Posture** when you have Microsoft 365 read access. An account with only Storefront read access is directed to **Licenses** for Adobe inventory; the other Cloud tabs are hidden.

## Choose the company and provider

Start with the company selector in the top bar. Company users can review only the company data their account permits. MSP staff may be able to select one or several companies.

Within license inventory, use the table's **Provider** and **Company** filters where shown. Provider is omitted when only one provider is available. Company is omitted when the top bar is already scoped to one company. Filters are specific to their page; recheck them after switching header tabs.

For account and license reviews, start with these guides:

- [Read cloud user accounts](/user-guides/cloud/users): account fields, security-data limits, and column preferences.
- [Find and filter cloud users](/user-guides/cloud/find-users): names, email, disabled accounts, and license filters.
- [Export cloud users](/user-guides/cloud/export-users): all matching users and the CSV field list.

- [Read cloud license usage](/user-guides/cloud/licenses): provider differences, capacity, renewal dates, and permissions.
- [Find, filter, and export licenses](/user-guides/cloud/filter-licenses): name/SKU filters, hidden allocations, spare seats, and CSV.
- [View license assignments and combinations](/user-guides/cloud/view-license-assignments): Microsoft and Google license holders, domains, offices, and combinations.

## Access and missing information

Microsoft 365 read access governs Microsoft and Google Cloud data. Adobe license inventory uses Storefront read access. Company administrators should contact their MSP to change user permissions or connect a service.

A missing tab, empty table, or unavailable value does not prove that the company has no service or no issue. Check the company selection and active filters. Ask your MSP to check permissions, provider connections, company mappings, and synchronization when information is still missing.

These pages display synchronized information. Check the relevant date or **Last synced** field where available, and confirm important changes with your MSP. Switching tabs does not trigger a provider synchronization.

MSP staff manage connected services in **Settings > Integrations**. See [Integrations](/user-guides/integrations/index) for provider setup guides.
