The **AI Assistant** is a conversational helper built into MSPortal. Instead of clicking through menus, you can simply describe what you need, and the assistant opens tickets, checks on and replies to existing tickets, searches your data, checks your training progress, and points you to the right page, all in plain language.

The assistant always acts as **you**. It can only see and do what your MSPortal permissions allow, and for client users it stays scoped to your own company.

## Where to find it

For client users (and for MSPs who have the AI home enabled), the assistant is your **Home** page. After signing in you land on a chat screen with a greeting and a prompt box.

You'll see:

- A time-based greeting (**Good morning / afternoon / evening**) followed by your first name and **"what can I help with?"**
- A prompt box that reads **"Ask anything about [your MSP]..."**
- A row of suggested actions to get you started.

### Opening a ticket, step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    Tell the assistant what's wrong, for example *"My laptop won't connect to the VPN."* You don't need to know which form or category to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer any follow-up questions">
    The assistant gathers any missing details through the conversation and builds a draft ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the draft">
    The assistant shows you the ticket it's about to create so you can check it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm to submit">
    Nothing is submitted until you confirm. Once you approve, the ticket is created and you'll get its details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
The assistant chooses the best ticket type automatically from your description, so you don't pick a template by name. The more clearly you describe the issue, the better the match.
</Note>

### Asking about an existing ticket

You don't need the ticket number to check on a ticket. Describe it the way you remember it, for example *"What's the status of my new computer request?"* or *"Any update on the printer issue?"* The assistant finds the matching ticket and answers from its actual updates, not just the status field, so you get the latest word from the technician working on it.

When the assistant searches for a ticket, it matches on:

- **Ticket title**, so "the printer issue" finds a ticket titled "Office printer offline"
- **Ticket number**, if you have it
- **Company name**, useful when you work across multiple companies

Closed tickets are searchable too, so you can ask about something that was already resolved, like *"How was my VPN ticket fixed last month?"*

### Replying to a ticket

You can respond to a ticket without leaving the chat. Say something like *"Tell them Thursday works for me"* or *"Let the tech know it's still broken."*

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask the assistant to reply">
    Name the ticket and say what you want to communicate, in your own words.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the exact text">
    The assistant shows you the exact note it's about to post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Nothing is posted until you approve the text. Once you confirm, the reply is added to the ticket where the technicians working on it will see it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
MSP staff can ask for a reply to be posted as an internal note that's hidden from the client. Replies from client users are always client-visible.
</Note>

### In the Chrome extension

The same ticket capabilities are available in the MSPortal Chrome extension's AI chat: ask about a ticket's status, search tickets, and post confirmed replies without opening the portal.

## Privacy and permissions

- The assistant runs with **your** permissions, never more.
- **Client users** can only access their own company's data. The assistant cannot reach other companies' records.
- Any action that creates or changes data is shown to you for confirmation first.

## Tips for better answers

<Tip>
Be specific. "Show me my open tickets from this month" works better than "tickets." Mentioning the system, error message, or timeframe helps the assistant find or create exactly what you need.
</Tip>

- Ask follow-up questions in the same conversation, the assistant remembers recent context.
- If a request is outside your access, the assistant will let you know rather than guess.
