**Audience:** Company users with Planner access and MSP staff. Company users see work made visible within their access scope. Your MSP manages permissions, templates and integration configuration.

Planner brings proposed work, dates, estimates and supporting records together. A Planner item does not by itself prove that work is approved, booked into a technician's calendar or completed.

## Timeline Views

Cards appear in the period containing their **due date**. Items without a due date are in **Unscheduled**. Check the year, company scope and filters if expected work is missing.

### Monthly View

Monthly shows January–December columns for the selected year. It is not a day-by-day calendar.

### Quarterly View

Quarterly groups work by quarter, displaying month ranges and years. Check the year when scrolling across consecutive years.

### Yearly View

Yearly uses one column per year. Scroll across the columns; its year arrows are disabled. **Today** returns to the current period without rescheduling work.

See [Read the timeline](/user-guides/planner/read-timeline) for navigation and recurring-cost projection.

## View Density

**View density** changes card detail. Comfortable shows the fullest detail; Compact hides expanded linked-record lists; Dense also hides status and priority badges. Hidden card detail does not remove the underlying data.

## Creating Planner Items

MSP staff with Planner write or manage permission can use **Planner Menu > List View > Add Item**. New items started from List View are hidden from Company users initially. Review the company, dates, status and **Visible to clients** before saving.

[Create and edit Planner items](/user-guides/planner/create-and-edit-items) explains the fields, Save and Discard Changes. [Update several items](/user-guides/planner/update-multiple-items) covers List View bulk actions and replacement of cost breakdowns.

## Importing PSA Opportunities

MSP staff with **Manage Planner** and a connected **Halo**, **ConnectWise** or **Autotask** integration can choose **Import** in the Planner header. The dialog selects opportunities already synchronized into MSPortal; it does not upload a spreadsheet.

<Warning>
Imported items are **client-visible by default**, subject to company access and Planner permissions. The import dialog has no visibility switch. Review the selected work and initial status mappings before importing, then check the created items.
</Warning>

[Import PSA opportunities](/user-guides/planner/import-opportunities) covers date filters, selection, status mapping, skipped records and verification. [Understand PSA-linked work](/user-guides/planner/understand-psa-linked-items) explains the resulting items for Company and MSP readers.

### Auto-Import on PSA Sync

An MSP can configure automatic opportunity import and field refresh in the PSA integration's **Opportunity Settings**. These settings are separate from the initial status mappings in the manual Import dialog. Turning off creation of new items does not by itself stop refresh of existing linked items. Ask the integration administrator which fields are controlled by the PSA.

### Creating a New Opportunity

Creating an opportunity from **Relationships > Create**, when available, is a separate external operation. Saving a Planner item does not automatically create a PSA opportunity. See [Link supporting records](/user-guides/planner/link-supporting-records) for the prerequisites and save boundaries.

## Reading and Editing Planner Items

Open an item's title in List View or on the timeline. Available sections and editing controls depend on access and the item's source.

**Notes** can be read by users with access to the item. **Private Notes (Internal Only)** are for authorized MSP staff. Company users do not receive private notes or the staff assignee picker.

Ordinary fields and notes require **Save**. File uploads and deletions happen immediately; Discard Changes does not undo those attachment operations. See [Read item details](/user-guides/planner/read-item-details) and [manage files](/user-guides/planner/manage-files).

## Using Templates

MSP staff with **Manage Planner** maintain reusable defaults under **Planner Menu > Templates**. Company users work with the resulting visible items.

- [Create and manage templates](/user-guides/planner/manage-templates).
- [Apply a template to one item](/user-guides/planner/apply-template-to-item), review replacements, then save the item.
- [Create items for multiple companies](/user-guides/planner/apply-templates-to-companies), which creates new items for selected template/company combinations.

## AI Features

### Ask AI

When AI is enabled and you have **Manage Planner**, open the Planner AI assistant. Describe the planning question or draft you need, including the intended company. Review any proposed changes before applying them. An assistant's message that it drafted something is not confirmation that a Planner item was saved; check the actual proposal and resulting item.

MSP staff can [ask Planner AI about planned work](/user-guides/planner/ask-planner-ai), check the answer's scope and completeness, then decide what to change.

MSP staff with Manage Planner and enabled AI can [draft a Planner item with AI](/user-guides/planner/draft-item-with-ai), review the proposal, and apply or discard it.

## Filtering and Search

[Filter the timeline](/user-guides/planner/filter-timeline) for visual planning, or use [List View filters](/user-guides/planner/find-planner-items) to locate specific rows. These views have different controls; changing the visible scope does not change the underlying item's company or status.
