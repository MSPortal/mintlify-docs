## How the tools work together

- **Client Health** combines observed service, engagement, financial, sales, project, standards, environment, and operational evidence into one portfolio view.
- **Ticket Trends** helps you investigate the ticket patterns behind Service Quality results and prepare client-facing talking points.
- **Standards** supplies the required-check deployment percentage used by the Standards dimension.
- **Financial Reporting** supplies the profitability evidence used by the Financial dimension.
- Planner items, goals, and compliance assignments contribute to the Operational dimension when real due-date or progress evidence exists.

Missing data is not treated as healthy. Client Health reduces evidence coverage when a source or metric is unavailable, and shows **Limited data** instead of a numeric score when the evidence is too thin.

## Data sources

| Data source | MSP Tools use |
|-------------|---------------|
| **PSA** | Tickets, invoices, opportunities, projects, revenue, and support effort |
| **RMM and device integrations** | Server availability, recognized patch state, and managed endpoint context |
| **Meetings and Surveys** | Meeting recency and recent NPS evidence |
| **Standards** | Required technology checks and company deployment status |
| **Planner, Goals, and Compliance** | Due-date timeliness and current goal progress |
| **Quoter data** | Sales activity and decided outcomes when PSA opportunities are unavailable |

<Note>
Each tool only uses data that is connected, mapped to the company, current enough for the calculation, and sufficient for the metric. A tenant-wide integration alone is not proof that a specific company has evidence.
</Note>

## Refresh schedule

Client Health publishes a complete scorecard each day at **2:00 AM UTC**. The dashboard, company detail, export, and history all read from that same published scorecard.

You can click **Refresh Scores** on the Client Health page after repairing an integration, updating a company mapping, changing scoring configuration, or making standards and operational changes that should be reflected immediately.

Ticket Trends prepares its weekly company aggregates on Monday before the next Client Health refresh. Other MSP Tools data follows the sync schedule of its source integration.

## Access MSP Tools

MSP Tools pages appear in the main sidebar when the feature and your role permissions allow access. Company portal users cannot access Client Health.

Configuration is available in **Settings** under **MSP Tools**:

- **Scoring** enables or disables Client Health dimensions
- **Standards** manages required categories and items
- **Financial Reporting** configures revenue classifications and targets

## Initial setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable MSP Tools">
    Confirm the required features are enabled for your tenant. Contact MSPortal support if an expected page is missing after permissions are assigned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and map integrations">
    Connect the PSA, RMM, and other sources you use. Verify that external companies are mapped to the correct MSPortal companies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Standards">
    Go to **Settings > MSP Tools > Standards** and define the required categories and items you want to track.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Financial Reporting">
    Go to **Settings > MSP Tools > Financial Reporting** and map revenue records so profitability metrics can be calculated.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review scoring dimensions">
    Go to **Settings > MSP Tools > Scoring** and disable only the dimensions that do not apply to your MSP's service model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the first scorecard">
    Open **Client Health** and click **Refresh Scores**, or wait for the daily 2:00 AM UTC refresh.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Permissions

| Permission | What it controls |
|-----------|------------------|
| `read_customer_success` | View Client Health and company health details |
| `write_customer_success` | Add, edit, and remove manual company risk flags |
| `read_ticket_trends` | View and run Ticket Trends analysis |
| `read_tool_stack` | View Standards deployment status |
| `manage_tool_stack` | Update company Standards status |
| `read_financial_reporting` | View Financial Reporting |
| `read_settings_client_health` | View Client Health scoring configuration |
| `manage_settings_client_health` | Enable or disable Client Health dimensions |
| `read_settings_tool_stack` | View Standards settings |
| `manage_settings_tool_stack` | Create and edit Standards categories and items |
| `read_settings_financial_reporting` | View Financial Reporting settings |
| `manage_settings_financial_reporting` | Modify revenue classification settings |

Assign permissions through **Settings > Users & Roles**.
