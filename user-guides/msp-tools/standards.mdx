**Audience:** MSP staff. Company users can [read Standards work shared in Planner](/user-guides/planner/understand-standards-items). The MSP manages the catalog, deployment choices, permissions and client visibility.

Standards, formerly Tool Stack, records deployment progress against your MSP's recommended tools and practices. It is available under **MSP Tools > Standards** when the feature and Standards read access are enabled.

## Read progress before changing it

The matrix has company rows and standard columns grouped into categories. It records **Deployed**, **In Progress**, **Not Started**, **N/A** and **Declined**. A deployed result can be unclassified or have a configured tier. Deployment progress and meeting a target tier are separate questions.

Required checks determine the deployment percentage. Optional standards appear separately, while a **1 of N** category counts as one check. Search and company scope change the displayed summary; excluded companies do not contribute even when shown. A percentage alone is not proof that every device is protected or every recommendation is deployed.

<Warning>
A column's **Mark all companies** action includes non-excluded companies hidden by the matrix search. Review the loaded company scope before any bulk action. Reading the matrix does not grant permission to change it.
</Warning>

[Read the Standards matrix](/user-guides/msp-tools/read-standards-matrix) explains the symbols, scoring, exclusions, menu scopes and troubleshooting.

## Standards and Planner

A standard can have a linked Planner item for each company. Click a linked matrix cell to open its item; a cell without a link does not create a task on click. The company-name link opens related Planner List View work with the **Standards** source filter.

Planner items can be shared with Company users subject to client visibility and access. They carry planning dates and estimates; they are not submitted support tickets, accepted quotes or confirmed appointments. See [Standards work in Planner](/user-guides/planner/understand-standards-items).

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are Standards and Tool Stack different modules?">
    Tool Stack is the former name. The current page, Planner source label and item badge say Standards. Older links can still contain Tool Stack.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where should Company users start?">
    Start with the Planner work the MSP shares. Company users do not normally have the MSP Tools sidebar switch or control the MSP's Standards catalog.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a deployed status prove current device health?">
    No. It records deployment progress. Verify live inventory and protection through the relevant device or security workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the company search restrict every bulk action?">
    No. A standard column's Mark all companies action covers all non-excluded companies in the loaded scope, including rows hidden by the search.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
