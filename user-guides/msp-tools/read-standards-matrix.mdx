**Audience:** MSP staff with Standards read access and the MSP Tools feature available. Company users should start with [Standards work shared in Planner](/user-guides/planner/understand-standards-items). Reading the matrix does not grant permission to change statuses or configure the catalog.

Standards records which tools or practices your MSP tracks for each company. Its deployment labels reflect the recorded status; they are not a live device scan, proof of subscription ownership or confirmation that every endpoint is protected.

## Find the company and standard

1. Open **MSP Tools > Standards**. If the entry is missing, ask your MSP administrator to check feature availability and Standards read access.
2. Check the company selector at the top of the app. It limits the loaded company scope.
3. Enter a company name in **Search companies...** inside the matrix.
4. Read across the company's row to the standard you need. Categories group the columns; scroll horizontally for more standards.
5. Clear the search to return to the other loaded companies.

Company rows can include indented child companies. Each row has its own recorded statuses; a parent's result does not automatically describe its child companies.

## Read the status symbols

| Display | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Green check: **Deployed, not classified** | Recorded as deployed, without a selected deployment tier. |
| Colored tier dot, when configured | Recorded as deployed using an option assigned to that tier. |
| Amber circle: **In Progress** | Deployment work is underway. |
| Gray X: **Not Started** | Deployment has not been marked as started. A missing tracking entry also appears this way. |
| Dash: **N/A** | The standard is not applicable to this company. |
| Red circled X: **Declined** | The standard was offered but declined. It is not a deployed result. |
| Italic column name with **\*** | Optional standard outside a one-of group. |

A green check without a tier is not the same as meeting a company's target tier. If tiers are configured, hover a colored status dot to read its selected option and tier. A red outline/warning indicates a deployed option below the company target; it remains deployed but needs a separate alignment review.

The tier badge next to a company name, when present, is its target. The MSP configures targets and deployment options in Standards settings. An absent badge does not prove that the company meets a target.

## Understand required checks and the percentage

The summary follows the **company search and current scope**. It is not necessarily the MSP's entire portfolio. Excluded companies do not contribute even when shown.

- In a normal category, each **Required** standard counts as one check unless it is **N/A** or **Declined**.
- A category marked **1 of N** contributes one check for the company. Any deployed member satisfies it; several deployed alternatives do not earn several required checks.
- If every member of a one-of group is N/A or Declined, that group has no applicable check for the company.
- Optional deployment counts are shown separately. They do not increase the required deployment percentage.

For example, three deployed checks, one In Progress check and one N/A check produce **3/4 required**, or **75% deployed**. The N/A check is outside the denominator.

<Note>
A higher percentage can result from marking work N/A, recording a decline or excluding a company, without deploying another tool. Read the status and exception counts alongside the percentage. **0/0 required** means there are no applicable required checks in that result; do not interpret its displayed percentage as a deployment failure rate.
</Note>

When tiers are configured, tier counts, **unclassified** and **below target** add detail to the deployed results. They answer a different question from “is it deployed?” A **1 of N** group still counts once, using its strongest deployed tier for the group's alignment.

Standards also contributes to Client Health. Compare scope, exclusions and refresh time before expecting a Client Health score to equal this matrix's current filtered percentage.

## Open the supporting Planner work

Click a status cell that has a linked Planner item to open that item. If the cell has no link, clicking does not create one. The MSP may need to review Standards synchronization.

Click the company name to open Planner List View for that company's **Standards** source items. Older links may use the former name **Tool Stack**. Access and client visibility still apply to the destination items.

Use [Read a Planner item](/user-guides/planner/read-item-details) to review its dates, status, notes and estimates. A Standards deployment record and its Planner task use different status labels; [Standards work in Planner](/user-guides/planner/understand-standards-items) explains the mapping.

## Inspect menus without changing data

The question-mark button beside the legend opens **Right-click actions**, which explains the menu scopes. Escape closes this help.

<Frame caption="The built-in help explains the three menu scopes. Selecting a status inside an action menu saves a change.">
  <img src="/images/product/standards-context-help.png" alt="Standards help explaining status-cell, company-row and standard-column actions." width="640" height="516" />
</Frame>

Right-clicking a status cell opens actions for one standard at one company. Right-clicking a company name opens actions for all its standards and company exclusion. Right-clicking a standard's column header opens actions for that standard across companies. Opening the menu does not save; choosing a status or exclusion action does.

<Warning>
**The company search does not limit a column's “Mark all companies” action.** That action covers every non-excluded company in the loaded company scope, including rows hidden by the search. Do not use a bulk menu as a way to inspect or filter statuses.
</Warning>

To review exclusions, use **Show N excluded** when it is available. Excluded rows appear faded with an **Excluded** badge. **Hide N excluded** hides them again. This display toggle does not re-include a company or change its records.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| The company is missing | Top-bar company scope, matrix search, active-company status and exclusions. |
| The standard is missing | Horizontal scrolling and whether the MSP has added it to a category. |
| A cell does not open Planner | It may not have a linked item, or you may lack access to that item. |
| A green status does not show a tier | It is deployed but unclassified; ask the MSP to review the selected deployment option. |
| The summary changed after searching | The summary is recalculated for the matching company rows. |
| The page says No standards configured unexpectedly | Confirm access and loading with the MSP; an unsuccessful read can also show this empty state. |
| A status disagrees with Planner | Reopen both records and ask the MSP to review synchronization; do not infer that one screenshot proves both are current. |

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is the Standards deployment matrix?">
    MSP staff open MSP Tools > Standards with the feature and Standards read access. Company users normally review the work shared in Planner instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Deployed prove the product is healthy on every device?">
    No. It is a recorded deployment status. Verify device health and protection in the relevant inventory or security workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Deployed, not classified mean?">
    The standard is recorded as deployed without a selected deployment tier. It does not by itself prove that the company's target tier is met.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a deployed standard still be below target?">
    Yes. A deployed option can be below the company's target tier. Deployment progress and alignment are separate measurements.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Declined the same as N/A?">
    No. Declined records an offered standard that was turned down; N/A means it does not apply. Neither is a deployed result, and both are excluded from applicable required checks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the 1 of N category badge mean?">
    Any one deployed member satisfies one required check for that company. Deploying several alternatives does not multiply the required-check credit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do optional standards increase the required deployment percentage?">
    No. Optional deployment counts are informational and shown separately from required checks.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the overall percentage change after a company search?">
    The matrix summary follows the currently matching company rows and excludes companies marked excluded. Clear the search to review the wider loaded scope.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a higher percentage always mean more tools were deployed?">
    No. Changes to N/A, Declined, exclusions or scope can change the denominator. Read exception counts and the deployed/required fraction too.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does 0/0 required mean?">
    No applicable required checks are present in that result. Its displayed percentage is not evidence that a set of required deployments failed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do child companies inherit their parent's displayed status?">
    No. Parent and child rows show their own recorded statuses. Indentation organizes the rows.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does searching for one company limit a column bulk update to that company?">
    No. Mark all companies applies across all non-excluded companies in the loaded company scope, including rows hidden by the matrix search.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Show excluded put companies back into scoring?">
    No. It only displays excluded rows. Re-including a company is a separate saved action.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does clicking a status cell do nothing?">
    A cell without a linked Planner item has no item to open. It does not create a task on click. Ask the MSP to review synchronization if one is expected.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can an older Standards link contain Tool Stack?">
    Tool Stack is the former name. Current Planner badges and source labels show Standards. An older link does not mean there is another catalog to configure.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does No standards configured always mean the catalog is empty?">
    No. An unsuccessful read can also lead to that display. Check access and loading before creating duplicate configuration.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
