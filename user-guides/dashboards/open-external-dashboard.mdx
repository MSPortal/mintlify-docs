**Audience:** Company users with dashboard access and MSP staff.

External dashboards display a service configured by your MSP. They may appear inside MSPortal or open in a separate browser tab. The service can require its own account and permissions.

## Open the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the dashboard">
    Open **Dashboards** and choose the external dashboard from the dashboard sidebar. A pinned dashboard may also have its own main-sidebar link. If it is missing, ask your MSP to check the company assignment, active status and role access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the company">
    Company users use the URL assigned to their company. MSP staff should select one company when reviewing that company’s dashboard; **Global View** uses the default URL. Do not treat a multi-company selection as a combined external report.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View the external service">
    Read the embedded dashboard when it loads. If MSPortal displays a blocked or pending message, use **Open in New Tab** when available. Complete any sign-in required by the external service.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<img src="/images/product/external-dashboard-open-control.png" alt="Open in New Tab control on an external dashboard." width="344" height="80" />

The image shows the fallback control in an MSP view. Company access still depends on the dashboard configuration.

<Note>A company needs its own assigned URL. The default URL is not a fallback for an unassigned company. A sidebar pin or default-dashboard setting does not grant access.</Note>

## Understand the result

| What you see | What it means and what to do |
| --- | --- |
| Embedded dashboard | Use the external service’s own filters and controls. Check the company and dates shown by that service. |
| Frame policy check pending | The portal has no completed embedding decision. Open in a new tab if available, or ask the MSP to check the policy. |
| Embedding blocked | The website may prevent display inside another site. Use the separate-tab option. |
| Sign-in screen | Authenticate with the external service using your authorized account. MSPortal access does not create that account. |
| Blank content or loading error | Ask the MSP to verify the URL, service availability and embedding support. A disappearing spinner is not proof that the content loaded. |
| Dashboard missing or not found | Ask the MSP to check active status, company URL and role access. Do not substitute another company’s link. |

## Filters and exports

The portal’s internal dashboard date, widget-width and PDF controls do not apply to an external dashboard. Any filtering, printing or downloading inside the external service follows that service’s capabilities and permissions.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where do I open an external dashboard?">
    Open Dashboards and choose its sidebar entry, or use its pinned main-sidebar link when available.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the dashboard missing for my company?">
    Ask the MSP to check the dashboard is active, your role is eligible and your company has an assigned URL. Company users do not manage these settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the default URL work for every company?">
    No. Company context requires that company’s assigned URL. The default URL is used for MSP global viewing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Company Admin access let me assign dashboards or change user permissions?">
    No. Ask the MSP to manage dashboard configuration and user permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why must I sign in again?">
    The external service may require its own login and access rights. Signing in to MSPortal does not replace them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does frame policy check pending mean?">
    The portal does not yet have a completed embedding decision. Use Open in New Tab if available or ask the MSP to run a policy check.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I do if embedding is blocked?">
    Use Open in New Tab when offered. The external website controls whether browsers may embed it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Force Allow Embedding bypass the provider’s restrictions?">
    No. It only changes the portal’s embedding decision. Browser restrictions and external-service authentication still apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Will selecting several companies combine their dashboards?">
    Do not assume that. Select one company to review its assigned external dashboard; multi-company selection is not an external-report aggregation tool.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use the internal dashboard PDF export here?">
    No. Use export or print controls supplied by the external service, if available. Internal-dashboard PDF and date controls do not operate on external content.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a sidebar link grant access?">
    No. A pin helps navigation; active status, company assignment, role access and external-service permissions still apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is the page blank after the loading spinner disappears?">
    The content may still be blocked or unavailable. Ask the MSP to verify the URL and external service; the spinner ending does not confirm successful loading.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
