**Audience:** Company users and MSP staff. Your notifications and available actions depend on your access. An approval requires an authorized reviewer; seeing a notification does not grant permission to approve it.

Open **Notifications** in the sidebar, or use the bell in the portal header for recent items.

## Two different tasks

| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | Shows recent alerts, including information, warnings, errors, success messages, product requests, and change approvals. |
| Pending Approvals | Shows automation approval requests and their recent history. Use the status filter to focus on pending decisions. |

The notification badge counts unread alerts. The approval badge counts pending requests. Reading an alert does not approve, reject, or complete the related workflow.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can Company users manage automation rules here?">
    No. Notifications is for reading alerts and, when assigned and authorized, reviewing a pending action. The MSP configures automation rules and reviewer assignments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Mark all read approve pending requests?">
    No. It changes notification read status. An approval needs a separate explicit decision.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Approved mean an email was sent?">
    No. Approved records the review decision. Execution follows separately; email approval history can show **Sent** when execution is recorded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I change product update emails?">
    Go to **Settings > User Settings > Email Notifications**. The product-update preference is separate from reading portal alerts. See [Manage your profile](/user-guides/account/manage-your-profile).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
