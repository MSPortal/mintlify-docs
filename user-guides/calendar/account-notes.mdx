**Audience:** MSP staff. Account Notes and QBR Log are internal views. Company users can [read shared meeting information](/user-guides/calendar/read-meeting-workspace) and [understand a meeting summary](/user-guides/calendar/understand-meeting-summary).

Account Notes combine portal-authored notes, structured QBR entries and synchronized Autotask Company Notes for one company. QBR entries record **Discussed**, **Agreed** and **Actioned** information. They are separate from the meeting's public notes, action-item list and summary email.

## Choose a starting point

| Task | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Read or write company account history | **Settings > Companies > company > Notes**. Select Notes explicitly if a link opens Overview. |
| Work with a meeting's QBR record | Open a company-linked meeting and choose **QBR Log**. Other QBR entries for its company can also appear. |
| See a short account-history preview | The company overview's **Account Notes** card. Its counts are not a total of every open company commitment. |
| Send a recap to attendees | The separate [Send Summary workflow](/user-guides/calendar/send-meeting-summary). |

## Generate a QBR entry from a meeting

On the meeting's **QBR Log**, choose **Generate from this meeting** when no linked entry exists. This is an AI generation and save action. The entry is not an unsaved preview waiting for another Save.

The generator can use meeting notes, agenda information, action items, transcript material and recent QBR history for the company. Automatic generation after an email can also use the sent summary. Check the resulting Discussed, Agreed and Actioned sections; edit inaccuracies deliberately.

Use **Regenerate with AI** on an existing meeting entry only when you intend to replace its contents. The confirmation describes the replacement. Completed ticks are preserved only for commitments whose descriptions still match after normalization; a reworded or omitted commitment is not guaranteed to keep its previous completion state.

<Warning>
Regeneration can overwrite handwritten edits and can update an Autotask copy that was previously linked. Check the entry and its destinations before confirming. The editor's ordinary push checkbox does not control this separate AI regeneration action.
</Warning>

AI generation attempts to add new commitments as meeting action items. This is best effort, and existing matching descriptions are skipped. A matching unique staff name can become an assignment; an unmatched name remains descriptive text. Verify the action list separately after generation. Later QBR edits or Done ticks do not continuously synchronize the separate action items.

## Automatic QBR entries after sending a summary

**Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting > Update the QBR log automatically** controls the automatic path. An eligible sent summary can start background generation for its company-linked meeting. **Writing the log entry** is a progress message, not proof that the entry was saved.

Automatic generation is skipped for meetings without a company, technician visits, service calls, internal meetings, disabled automatic logging and linked entries already edited by hand. A new manually created entry is not protected solely by its manual origin; the protection uses a later recorded edit. Manual regeneration is an explicit replacement path with different rules.

Email acceptance, QBR generation, meeting action creation and PSA synchronization are separate outcomes. If the log is missing, check the settings, meeting type, existing entry and any error before starting another generation.

## Autotask copies

Portal notes can exist without any PSA connection. Optional Company Note writeback in this workflow is for **Autotask**, subject to the company's mapping and available note types.

For ordinary manual saves, choose a note type and deliberately enable the push/update checkbox. Automatic QBR writeback has its own **Push QBR log entries to Autotask as** setting. Leaving it on **Do not push to Autotask** keeps that automatic writeback disabled.

Deleting a portal note does not delete its Autotask copy. A successful portal save does not prove that a provider update succeeded. See the [editing guide](/user-guides/calendar/write-account-notes) for retry and deletion behavior.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are Account Notes the same as public meeting notes?">No. Account Notes and QBR Log are internal company history. Public meeting notes, meeting action items and the summary email are separate records and workflows.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need AI to record a QBR?">No. Use Add note and choose QBR log in company Notes, or Add entry on a meeting's QBR Log tab when no linked entry exists.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Generate from this meeting only make an unsaved preview?">No. It generates and saves the QBR entry and can attempt related action-item creation. Review the saved result.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does regeneration preserve every Done tick?">No. Preservation depends on matching commitment descriptions. Reworded or omitted commitments may not retain their ticks.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can regeneration update an existing Autotask copy?">Yes. A previously linked copy can be updated by the separate generation path. The ordinary edit dialog's push checkbox does not govern that action.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does creating a manual QBR automatically create meeting action items?">No. Ordinary manual save does not mirror commitments. AI generation has a separate best-effort action-creation step; check its results independently.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does sending a summary guarantee a QBR entry was written?">No. Automatic logging depends on settings, meeting type, company association and existing edits, and background work can fail. Verify the entry itself.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are handwritten entries always protected from automatic replacement?">Protection applies to a recorded later manual edit, including a saved commitment-status change. A newly created manual entry is not protected just by having manual origin.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
