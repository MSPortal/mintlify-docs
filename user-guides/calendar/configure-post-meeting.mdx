**Audience:** MSP administrators with Calendar settings access and permission to manage these settings. Company users do not configure this workflow; they can [read a meeting summary](/user-guides/calendar/understand-meeting-summary).

Open **Settings > Calendar > Post-Meeting**. These settings apply across your MSP tenant, rather than to one company or your personal calendar.

The **Post-Meeting Flow** card controls the workflow. The **Post-meeting survey questions** card has separate [question editing and save controls](/user-guides/calendar/manage-meeting-survey-questions).

## Choose when wrap-up is available

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the flow">
    Turn on **Enable post-meeting flow** to offer the internal survey, transcript and summary workflow to authorized staff.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select meeting types">
    Open **Event Types** and select the types that should offer wrap-up. The choices are **QBR**, **On-site Tech Visit**, **Internal Meeting**, **Client Call**, **Project Milestone**, **Compliance Review**, **Business Review**, **Service Call** and **Other**. Keep at least one selected. To disable wrap-up entirely, turn off **Enable post-meeting flow** instead of trying to clear every type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the completion prompt">
    **Prompt when a meeting is completed** controls the invitation to start wrap-up when a meeting is marked Completed. Turning this off does not itself disable the enabled wrap-up workflow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the settings">
    Select **Save** in the page header. Wait for the save to finish, then revisit Post-Meeting to check the saved values. The button is disabled when there are no pending settings changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Switching settings tabs is not a save or a reliable way to discard a draft. Save the intended changes before leaving the page; reload to return to the saved settings if you want to discard unsaved flow controls.

## Understand each control

| Control | What it controls | What to check separately |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Enable post-meeting flow** | Availability of the internal wrap-up workflow. | Meeting type and staff access. |
| **Event Types** | Which meeting types offer wrap-up. | This selection does not define automatic QBR eligibility. |
| **Prompt when a meeting is completed** | The completion prompt. | Staff can still use enabled wrap-up entry points without the prompt. |
| **AI transcript processing** | Generating notes and action items from a stored transcript in wrap-up. | Transcript storage and summary drafting are separate. |
| **Update the QBR log automatically** | Automatic company QBR logging after an eligible summary is sent. | Company linkage, meeting type, an existing manually edited entry, and any generation failure. |
| **Push QBR log entries to Autotask as** | The Autotask Company Note type for eligible automatic QBR writeback, when available. | It does not send a summary email or push a note merely by saving this setting. |

All controls in this table use the header **Save**. Turning off the main flow does not automatically turn off the other switches. If you want to stop automatic QBR logging, turn off **Update the QBR log automatically** explicitly and save.

## Configure transcript AI

Turn **AI transcript processing** on or off and save. Staff can still store a transcript when transcript AI is off. Generating transcript notes, applying them to a meeting, and generating a summary are different actions.

Turning this setting off is not a global switch for every AI feature. See [Add a meeting transcript](/user-guides/calendar/add-meeting-transcript) for storage, generated output and **Add to meeting** behavior.

## Configure automatic QBR logging

1. Set **Update the QBR log automatically** as needed.
2. For an available Autotask connection, review **Push QBR log entries to Autotask as**. Choose a note type to enable that writeback, or **Do not push to Autotask** to keep it off.
3. Select the header **Save**.
4. After an eligible summary is sent, check the company's [Account Notes and QBR Log](/user-guides/calendar/read-account-notes) for the actual result.

Automatic QBR generation excludes Internal Meeting, On-site Tech Visit and Service Call meetings. It also needs a linked company. An existing QBR entry that staff have manually edited is protected from automatic replacement. Enabling every wrap-up event type does not remove these restrictions.

The Autotask type selector is disabled when automatic QBR logging is off. Turning logging off can retain the previously selected type; review it before enabling logging again.

## Troubleshooting

- **I can open the settings but Save fails:** viewing Calendar settings and managing the flow are separate permissions. Ask an MSP administrator to review your role. Question editing has its own Calendar/Meetings management requirement.
- **Wrap-up is missing:** check the saved flow switch, the meeting's selected event type and your meeting permissions. Company users do not use internal wrap-up.
- **No completion prompt:** check its separate switch and whether the prompt was dismissed for this meeting.
- **No QBR entry appeared:** check eligibility, company linkage, manual edits and the actual generation outcome. A sent email does not prove the QBR or provider copy succeeded. Do not resend the email just to repair a missing QBR entry.
- **No Autotask note-type selector:** it appears only when an applicable Autotask connection and its note-type context are available.
