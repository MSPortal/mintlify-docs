**Audience:** Company users with access to a meeting and MSP staff. Your MSP assigns access to the meeting, its company and linked records. Meeting management requires additional permissions.

Open a meeting's **...** menu in Calendar List View or My Meetings and choose **Open Meeting Page**. The full workspace is separate from the Calendar meeting dialog.

## Know which actions save changes

Meeting notes require **Save Notes**; they do not autosave. Each notes tab saves separately. Selecting **Status**, changing agenda visibility, dragging an item or removing it can save independently of notes. Choosing a file starts its separate upload workflow.

The attendee panel is a reader, not an attendee editor or attendance-confirmation report. The current **Edit Meeting Details** header button does not open an editor in the verified workflow. Use the Calendar meeting dialog when permitted, or ask your MSP for schedule or attendee changes.

## MSP follow-up workflows

**Present**, **Wrap-Up** and **Send Summary** are separate workflows. Wrap-Up appears for MSP staff when enabled for the meeting type. Opening the meeting page does not automatically perform these actions.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where is Open Meeting Page?">
    Find the meeting in List View or My Meetings and open its row menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are notes automatically saved?">
    No. Use Save Notes when authorized; it saves the current notes tab.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I manage attendees in the workspace's attendee panel?">
    No. Use the Calendar meeting dialog when permitted or ask your MSP.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the page have one Save button for every action?">
    No. Status, agenda and file actions can save separately from notes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does reading the workspace send a meeting summary?">
    No. Send Summary is a separate workflow.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do all quote providers have the same meeting-agenda support?">
    No. The agenda's Quote link currently uses Quoter; the separate Quotes module supports Salesbuildr, QuoteWerks and Quoter.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
