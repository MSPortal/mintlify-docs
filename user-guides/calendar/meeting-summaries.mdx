---
title: Meeting Summaries
description: Wrap up a meeting with an internal survey, transcript notes, and an AI-drafted client summary
---

After a meeting, the post-meeting wrap-up walks your team through three steps: an internal survey, the transcript and the AI notes drawn from it, and the client summary email. You can stop at any point and pick the wrap-up back up later.

You can also open **Send Summary** on its own if all you need is the email.

## Starting the wrap-up

When a meeting is marked **Completed**, MSPortal offers to start the wrap-up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer the prompt">
    **Wrap up this meeting?** appears with **Start wrap-up**, **Not now**, and **Don't ask for this meeting**. Choosing **Not now** leaves the wrap-up available from the meeting page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue later at any time">
    A meeting with a wrap-up in progress shows a **Post-meeting wrap-up in progress** banner with a **Continue wrap-up** link. **Save & exit** stores your progress on every step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Whether staff are prompted when a meeting is completed, and whether AI transcript processing is available, are controlled in **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting**.
</Note>

## Step 1: Survey

An internal rating of how the meeting went. Answers are visible only to your team, never to the client.

Question types include a 1-5 rating, a 1-10 scale, Yes / No, a choice list, and free text. Questions marked **Required** must be answered before you continue. If no questions are configured, the step tells you so and you can skip it.

<Tip>
Build the question set in **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting**. **Add default questions** starts you off with client engagement, meeting value, client sentiment, follow-up needed, and additional comments.
</Tip>

## Step 2: Transcript & AI

Add the meeting transcript and let AI draft notes and action items from it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the transcript">
    Upload a `.txt`, `.vtt`, `.srt`, or `.md` file up to 5 MB, or paste the text and click **Use pasted text**. You can **Replace transcript** or **Remove** it later; removing it also clears the AI output generated from it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate notes and action items">
    Click **Generate notes & action items**. AI reads the transcript and drafts meeting notes plus a list of action items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before anything is saved">
    Nothing reaches the meeting until you apply it. Edit the drafted notes, remove action items you do not want, and set an **Assignee**, **Due date**, and priority on the ones you keep.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add to meeting">
    Clear **Add these notes to the meeting notes** if you only want the action items. Click **Add to meeting** to write your selection to the meeting record.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
If AI is not available for your account, the transcript is still saved with the meeting. It continues to feed the summary draft in step 3.
</Note>

## Step 3: Send Summary

The summary step drafts the client email from everything the meeting now holds.

### The AI draft

The draft starts automatically when you reach this step in the wrap-up. On the standalone Send Summary page, click **Generate with AI** instead.

The **AI draft** panel shows what the draft was built from under **Based on**: **Meeting notes**, **Agenda** with an item count, **Action items** with a count, and **Transcript**. When the meeting has none of these, the panel says so up front rather than leaving you to guess why the draft is thin.

Two controls shape the output:

| Control | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Length** | Brief, Standard, Detailed |
| **Tone** | Friendly, Neutral, Executive |

Change either one and click **Regenerate** to redraft.

<Tip>
The greeting is taken from your **To** list and the sign-off from your own profile, so neither is asked for. Review the draft before sending: AI writes from the meeting record, and anything the record does not contain will not be in the email.
</Tip>

### Recipients and subject

The compose header holds **To**, **CC**, and **Subject** on one line each. CC stays hidden until you use it.

Start typing in **To** or **CC** to search people. The list merges meeting participants, assigned staff, and the company's contacts. Use the arrow keys and press Enter or Tab to add someone, Backspace to remove the last one. Anyone outside the list can be added by typing their full email address.

The subject defaults to **Meeting Summary: [meeting title]** and is editable.

### Editing the content

The draft opens in a rich text editor. Edit any part of it, add or remove sections, format with bold, italic, and lists, and insert links.

### Linking to a PSA ticket

With a PSA integration connected (ConnectWise, Autotask, or Halo PSA), you can file the summary on a ticket at the same time:

1. Turn on **Add as note to [PSA] ticket**
2. Search by ticket number or summary and select the ticket
3. Sending the summary adds it to that ticket as a note

Tickets are scoped to the meeting's company, so if nothing is found, check that the company's tickets are synced from your default PSA.

### Sending

Review the recipients, subject, and content, then click **Send Email**. The footer shows the recipient count and the account the email is sent from.

If you do not need to send anything, **Finish without sending** completes the wrap-up.

## What happens after you send

- The wrap-up is marked complete. You can still reopen any step to review or update it.
- If **Update the QBR log automatically** is on, MSPortal writes a QBR log entry for the company and opens the meeting's **QBR Log** tab. The entry takes a few seconds to appear, and the tab shows that it is being written. See [Account Notes and QBR Log](/user-guides/calendar/account-notes).
- Commitments captured in that entry are added to the meeting's action items, so follow-ups are chased where your team already works.

## Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Capture notes during the meeting">
    The draft is built from what the meeting record holds. Notes taken during the meeting produce a noticeably better summary than a transcript alone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Build comprehensive agendas">
    The more agenda items you include, the more context there is to work with. Planner items, budgets, and compliance checks all contribute to richer summaries.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Review before sending">
    Always read through the draft. Check figures, names, and commitments against what was actually agreed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Use PSA integration">
    Link summaries to PSA tickets to keep a complete record of client communications. This helps with continuity when team members change or when reviewing account history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Send promptly">
    Send summaries within 24 hours of the meeting while the discussion is still fresh. Prompt follow-up demonstrates professionalism and attention to detail.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Summary Templates

The draft adapts to the meeting type:

| Meeting Type | Summary Focus |
|--------------|---------------|
| **QBR** | Comprehensive review of the quarter, metrics, achievements, and upcoming initiatives |
| **Strategy** | Key decisions, roadmap items, and strategic priorities |
| **Check-in** | Status updates, quick wins, and immediate action items |
| **Onboarding** | Setup progress, training completed, and next steps |
