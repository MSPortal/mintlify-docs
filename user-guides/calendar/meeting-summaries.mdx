## Choose a starting point

| Entry | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Send Summary** | Opens the standalone composer. It does not automatically generate or send an email. |
| **Wrap-Up** / **Start wrap-up** / **Continue wrap-up** | Opens the internal post-meeting flow. The first visit can create its progress record. |
| Wrap-up **Summary** step | Can automatically request an initial AI draft when AI is available. Review before sending. |

Wrap-up availability and the completion prompt depend on **Settings > Operations > Calendar > Post-Meeting** and the meeting type. **Not now** defers the prompt. **Don't ask for this meeting** is a separate dismissal choice.

## Know what is saved

The wrap-up contains **Survey**, **Transcript & AI**, and **Send Summary** steps. Each has its own save behavior; changing steps is not a universal save.

- Submit survey answers with **Continue**. Unsaved answer changes are not preserved just by leaving the step. If no questions have ever been configured, built-in defaults can appear; disabling every configured question leaves no active questions.
- Selecting a transcript file uploads/saves it; pasted text needs **Use pasted text**. Generating AI notes saves generated output in the wrap-up; **Add to meeting** separately applies reviewed notes/actions to the meeting.
- The unsent email's body, recipients and subject remain in the open page only. **Save & exit** does not persist them. Keep a separate copy before leaving or reloading.

Opening **Summary** can generate AI content. **Send Email** is the deliberate sending action; **Finish without sending** completes the wrap-up without sending an email. Neither is a substitute for checking the meeting's own status and action items.

## Check follow-up separately

After sending, verify the email result and any optional public PSA ticket note separately. Eligible automatic QBR logging, commitment creation and configured QBR PSA synchronization happen independently and can fail or be skipped. See the [sending guide](/user-guides/calendar/send-meeting-summary) for the exact distinctions.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need the whole wrap-up to send a meeting summary?">No. Use Send Summary on a company-linked meeting page for the standalone composer.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Save & exit save every field in wrap-up?">No. Unsubmitted survey answers and unsent email edits can be lost. Use each step's explicit save action and keep a separate copy of an unsent email.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening wrap-up only read the meeting?">No. The first visit can create a progress record, and entering Summary can generate an AI draft when enabled.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Finish without sending send a recap?">No. It completes the wrap-up without sending the summary email.</Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do Company users fill in the internal wrap-up survey?">No. The post-meeting wrap-up is for MSP staff. Company recipients can review the summary email and ask their MSP for corrections.</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
