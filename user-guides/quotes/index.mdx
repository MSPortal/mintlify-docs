Open **Quotes** to find quotes from **Quoter**, **QuoteWerks**, and **Salesbuildr** that your MSP has made available through its connected integrations. Company users can review accessible quotes and open the provider’s quote page when a link is available. MSP staff configure integrations and Company access.

## Supported quoting providers

| Provider | Available quote workflow |
| --- | --- |
| **Quoter** | Find synchronized quotes and open the linked Quoter proposal to review the current document and available acceptance options. |
| **QuoteWerks** | Find synchronized quotes and open their customer-facing link, including QuoteValet when configured. |
| **Salesbuildr** | Find synchronized quotes, open the linked proposal, and request an available PDF through Download PDF. |

Imported pricing and line-item details depend on the data supplied by the provider. A missing external link or PDF button does not mean Quoter or QuoteWerks is unsupported. Follow the [document guide](/user-guides/quotes/download-quotes) for the correct provider path.

Creating, revising, pricing, and publishing quotes takes place in the connected quoting system. The Quotes list displays synchronized records; it is not a quote editor. Availability and freshness depend on the provider, company mapping, synchronization, and your permissions.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which quoting systems does Quotes support?">
    Quoter, QuoteWerks, and Salesbuildr. Your MSP connects the providers it uses and controls which synchronized quotes you can access. The built-in PDF action is specific to Salesbuildr; that does not limit quote support to Salesbuildr.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do I find a quote from my IT provider?">
    Open **Quotes**. Find the quote and select its linked **Number**, or use its **…** menu’s **Open in** provider action when available. See [find and open a quote](/user-guides/quotes/view-quotes).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I create or change a quote here?">
    The portal list and detail page are for reviewing imported quotes. Ask your MSP to create or revise the quote in its quoting system.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator give me access to more quotes?">
    Your MSP manages portal users, permission roles, and quote visibility. Ask your IT provider to check your access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening a quote accept it?">
    Opening the portal record or provider link does not itself accept the quote. Review any acceptance controls and terms on the provider’s page before taking that separate action.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
