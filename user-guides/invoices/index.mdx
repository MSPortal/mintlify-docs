**Invoices** shows invoice records synchronized from the MSP's PSA. Company users can review the invoices available to their company and role. MSP staff also manage the integrations and access that make these records available.

| Task | Guide |
| --- | --- |
| Find an invoice, inspect its status, or add it to a meeting | [View invoices](/user-guides/invoices/view-invoices) |
| Read a PDF, download PDFs in a ZIP, or export a CSV | [Download and export invoices](/user-guides/invoices/download-invoices) |
| Open an available payment link | [Use an invoice payment link](/user-guides/invoices/pay-an-invoice) |
| MSP: inspect Pax8 company charges and drafts | [Review Pax8 invoice charges](/user-guides/invoices/view-pax8-invoices) |
| MSP: configure the payment-link integration | [Set up BenjiPays](/user-guides/integrations/enable-benjipays-integration) |

## Access and data sources

Your MSP assigns invoice access and connects the source system. The PSA invoice list supports ConnectWise, Autotask, and Halo. Available columns, invoice statuses, document availability, and update timing depend on that source and the MSP's configuration.

When Pax8 is connected, MSP staff may also see a separate **Pax8** tab. Company users use the PSA invoice list; the Pax8 tab is not their billing portal.

Viewing an invoice does not edit its source record, mark it paid, or create a payment. Use the payment provider's confirmation to establish whether a payment succeeded, and allow for the source invoice data to update.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can a Company administrator grant invoice access?">
    Your MSP manages portal users and permissions. Ask the MSP to review your invoice access and company assignment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I create or edit a PSA invoice here?">
    The invoice list is a view of synchronized records. It has no invoice-creation or amount-editing form. Contact the MSP about a source invoice correction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does opening an invoice mark it as paid?">
    No. Reading or downloading an invoice is separate from paying it. An available payment button opens the configured payment provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I add an invoice directly to Planner from its row menu?">
    The current row menu offers View PDF and Add to Meeting. It does not offer Add to Planner. Arrange a separate follow-up task through the available planning workflow.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
