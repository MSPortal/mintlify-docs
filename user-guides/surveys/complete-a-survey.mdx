Open the questionnaire link sent by your IT provider. The invitation opens the survey directly; you do not need permission to create surveys or administer the portal to respond.

<Note>
For survey recipients. Your invitation identifies the response it accepts, so use the link addressed to you. Do not assume that an emailed questionnaire is anonymous.
</Note>

## Answer and submit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your invitation">
    Follow the survey link in the provider's message. Check the survey title and introduction so you know what feedback is being requested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer the questions">
    Complete questions marked with an asterisk. Depending on the questionnaire, answers can use text, single or multiple choices, numbers, dates, yes/no controls, or rating scales.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your response">
    Check your answers before submitting. A checkmark beside a field indicates that an answer is present; it does not mean the whole survey has been sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the questionnaire">
    Select **Submit Survey** and wait for the result. Correct any highlighted required-field errors. A successful submission displays a thank-you message.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After submission, the invitation is marked completed. There is no recipient-side edit button for the completed questionnaire. Contact the provider if you need to correct an answer.

## Leaving and returning

Complete and submit the survey before closing it when possible. Do not rely on automatic progress saving or expect a draft to appear on another device.

If the page offers **Remember progress on this device** and a saved-progress indicator, that is local browser-session progress, not a submitted response. **Clear saved progress** clears the locally retained answers. You still need to select **Submit Survey** to send the questionnaire.

## If the link or submission fails

| Message or situation | Next step |
| --- | --- |
| Invitation is invalid or unavailable | Reopen the complete original link or request a replacement from the provider |
| Invitation has expired | Ask the provider for a current invitation |
| Survey already completed | The link cannot accept another normal response; contact the provider about a correction |
| Required question is highlighted | Enter the requested answer and submit again |
| A valid answer is still rejected | Keep your intended answer and report the question and error to the provider; do not change your feedback merely to bypass the error |
| Submission outcome is uncertain | Reopen the original link or ask the provider to confirm receipt before attempting repeated submissions |

A questionnaire can be associated with your invitation, company, or support ticket. Depending on the provider's configuration, submitted feedback can be used in reports, trigger follow-up, or appear in the linked ticket history.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need to sign in or have survey-management permission?">
    The emailed invitation opens the public response form. Creating surveys and viewing management reports are separate capabilities.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does choosing an answer send it immediately?">
    No. Complete the form and select **Submit Survey**. A field checkmark is not submission confirmation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I leave and finish on another device?">
    Do not rely on that. Any available saved progress is local to the browser session. It is not a synchronized account draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Remember progress the same as submitting?">
    No. It retains local progress when available. The provider receives the questionnaire through **Submit Survey**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit my answers after submission?">
    The completed invitation has no recipient edit action. Ask the provider to arrange a correction or another invitation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a colleague's invitation?">
    Use your own invitation. Responses can be associated with the intended recipient and company; ask the provider for a separate link if needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my response anonymous?">
    Do not assume so. The invitation can associate the response with recipient and company information, and ticket-related feedback can appear in the linked ticket history.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I submit again after a network error?">
    First check whether the original link now reports completion or ask the provider to confirm receipt. An error or interrupted page does not always establish that nothing was saved.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
