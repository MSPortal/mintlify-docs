Use **Goals** to review objectives for your permitted company scope. Goals have a title, optional description, status, priority, category, progress, and target dates. Your MSP can configure the available choices and custom fields.

**Company users:** read goals shared with your company. Editing requires additional Goals management permission assigned by your MSP. A Company administrator title alone does not grant it.

**MSP staff:** maintain company or organization goals, control Company visibility, and connect goals to planning and meetings when permitted.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why can I view a goal but not edit it?">
    Reading and managing Goals are separate permissions. Ask your MSP to review your role; do not change your own permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are all goals visible to Company users?">
    No. Company readers see permitted company goals that are shared with them. Internal goals and organization goals without a company are not Company-facing.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a goal automatically become a task?">
    No. **Add to Planner** prepares a separate Planner item that must be reviewed and saved. The original goal remains.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I create a goal by opening its form?">
    The form is a draft until **Create Goal** succeeds. Editing an existing goal requires **Update Goal**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
