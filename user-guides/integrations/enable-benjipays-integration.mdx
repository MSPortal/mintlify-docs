BenjiPays setup adds a payment link to eligible unpaid invoice rows. MSPortal substitutes the invoice number and amount due into your template; payment happens on the destination website.

This guide is for **MSP staff with access to Integrations settings**. Changing or disconnecting the template requires integration management permission. Company users can use available invoice payment links, but do not administer this setup.

## Prepare the payment link

Obtain your merchant’s link through **Settings > Custom Payment Links** in Benji Pays, following its [custom payment link guide](https://support.benjipays.com/support/solutions/articles/150000181442-custom-payment-links-for-any-psa-or-erp-crm). Keep the merchant URL and any issued link parameters. Use MSPortal’s exact, case-sensitive placeholders for the invoice and amount values:

- `[INVOICENUMBER]`
- `[AMOUNTDUE]`

The URL must use `https://` and a `benjipays.com` or `benjipays.io` address, including their subdomains. Other systems’ placeholder names must be replaced with the two MSPortal placeholders above.

You also need invoice data available in MSPortal and the corresponding invoice/accounting data available to your BenjiPays merchant. This setup has no API credential form or invoice synchronization step.

## Configure and save

1. Open **Settings > Integrations**, find **BenjiPays**, and open its setup.
2. Paste the URL into **Payment link template**.
3. Optionally change **Button label**, up to 40 characters. The default is **Pay Now**.
4. Inspect **Live preview**. It uses sample invoice **INV-1024** and amount **499.00**, even before saving.
5. Choose **Save & enable** for initial setup, or **Save changes** when editing. Wait for the save result, then reopen Invoices to check the available payment buttons.

<Warning>
The preview button opens the actual configured external URL with sample values. It is not a payment sandbox or a validation-only control. Inspect the generated URL first; do not submit a payment as a documentation or setup check.
</Warning>

Editing fields changes the draft and preview. It does not persist the configuration until Save succeeds. **Cancel** leaves the form without saving that draft.

## Understand invoice buttons

The same saved template and label apply across the tenant. Invoice access still depends on the user’s permissions and company scope.

Voided or cancelled invoices do not receive a button. Otherwise, a positive available balance makes an invoice eligible. When no balance is available, the portal falls back to a positive invoice total unless the invoice is closed. The link uses the invoice number, with an external identifier as a fallback, and formats the amount to two decimal places.

The button opens the destination in a new tab. Opening it does not mark an invoice paid. Amounts reflect the invoice data currently available in MSPortal and can lag payment activity. Check the invoice/accounting synchronization when a balance looks stale.

BenjiPays merchant rules also matter. Its optional [amount-matching rule](https://support.benjipays.com/support/solutions/articles/150000185669-require-transaction-amount-on-pay-now-invoice-links-to-match-the-invoice-s-total) checks against the invoice total. MSPortal supplies the outstanding amount when available, so review compatibility for partially paid invoices rather than assuming every generated link will be accepted.

## Change or disconnect

Reopen setup, edit the template or label, and choose **Save changes**. The **Active** badge represents a saved configuration, not a successful checkout test.

Choose **Disconnect**, then confirm, to remove the MSPortal payment-link configuration. Reopen Invoices to verify the buttons disappear. This does not cancel the BenjiPays merchant account, remove invoices, or reverse payments.

## Troubleshooting

- **Save is disabled:** check the URL, both exact placeholders, label length, and your management access.
- **The preview works but Save fails:** the server additionally requires HTTPS and an allowed BenjiPays hostname. A clickable draft preview is not proof of accepted configuration.
- **No invoice button:** check invoice access, saved configuration, available invoice data, balance/status and whether the payment column is visible.
- **Wrong invoice or amount:** compare the source invoice number and current balance with the merchant/accounting records. Do not repeatedly submit a payment.
- **Destination rejects the link:** check the merchant URL, invoice availability and merchant payment-link rules with the provider.

See [Pay an invoice](/user-guides/invoices/pay-an-invoice) for the client workflow.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can configure BenjiPays?">
    MSP staff with Integrations settings access can open setup; saving and disconnecting require integration management permission. Company invoice access does not grant setup access.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does BenjiPays setup synchronize invoices?">
    No. It saves a tenant-wide payment-link template; invoice data comes from the portal’s invoice sources.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which placeholders are required?">
    Use the exact case-sensitive [INVOICENUMBER] and [AMOUNTDUE] placeholders in an HTTPS BenjiPays URL.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Live preview a sandbox?">
    No. It opens the configured external destination with sample values, even for an unsaved draft.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the Active badge prove payments work?">
    No. It indicates saved configuration. Invoice availability, current balances and merchant rules still determine whether checkout works.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does clicking Pay Now mark an invoice paid?">
    No. It opens the payment website. Payment and subsequent invoice updates are separate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I set a different merchant link per company here?">
    No. This setup saves one tenant-wide template and button label.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does Disconnect remove?">
    It removes the portal payment-link configuration. It does not cancel the merchant account or reverse payments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
