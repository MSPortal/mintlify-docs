**Audience:** MSP staff with Compliance settings access. Creating, editing and pausing schedules requires write access; deleting requires manage access. The Compliance feature must be available to your tenant.

Open **Settings > Compliance > Schedule**. This is tenant-wide configuration. The top Company selector does not limit a schedule to the company you happen to be viewing: its **Targets** determine who receives runs.

## Before creating a schedule

Prepare an active template under **Settings > Compliance > Runs** with the intended groups and individual checks. Review its contents before scheduling it. Only active templates appear in the schedule picker. A template defines the assessment package; a schedule decides when and for whom to create runs.

## Create the schedule

1. Select **Create Schedule**. Enter a recognizable **Schedule Name**, optional description, and **Run Template**.
2. Choose **Targets** deliberately. The new form starts with **All companies**, which targets active companies across the tenant. Choose **Specific companies** for an explicit client list, **Company groups** for the available groups, or **Industries** for company industry matches.
3. For a targeted mode, select at least one entry. The summary counts selected groups or industries in those modes; it is not a preview of the final company count. If no groups are offered, use Specific companies or review company classification; do not assume every PSA-synced type is a schedulable group.
4. Set the **Frequency** and the additional recurrence controls. Review the preview after changing frequency because its recurrence options reset to defaults for that frequency.
5. Choose the **Start date**, or **Run date** for a one-time schedule. The form offers dates and patterns, not an exact time-of-day or time-zone selector.
6. Select **Create Schedule** and wait for success. New schedules are created active. **Cancel** closes the unsaved draft.
7. Reopen the saved schedule with **Edit** to verify its template, target mode, selections and recurrence. Review resulting runs in Compliance when the schedule executes.

## Recurrence choices

| Frequency | Configure |
|---|---|
| One-time | Run date |
| Weekly | Day of the week and start date |
| Semi-monthly | Two offered days of the month; the second can be the last day |
| Monthly | A day of the month, last day, or an ordinal weekday such as the first Monday |
| Quarterly | Starting month and day pattern; repeats every three months |
| Semi-annual | Starting month and day pattern; repeats every six months |
| Annual | Month and day pattern |

Use the offered day choices rather than assuming an unavailable date is supported. A saved schedule is not proof that assessments already exist: background processing creates runs and then assigns checks. A processing failure or no eligible active companies can prevent expected results. No exact execution-time guarantee is implied by the date preview.

## Edit, pause and delete

- **Edit** opens a draft of the saved configuration. **Update Schedule** persists it; Cancel leaves it unchanged.
- The row's **Active/Paused** switch saves immediately. There is no separate page Save for the status switch. Pausing controls future scheduling; it does not undo assessments already created.
- **Delete** opens a confirmation. Confirm **Delete Schedule** only when future scheduling should stop. Removing a schedule is not a way to delete historical runs or completed evidence.

Review **Last Run** separately from completion of the resulting checks. A last-run timestamp does not certify that assignments finished or that a company passed its assessment.

## Troubleshooting

If Create Schedule is disabled, check the name, template and target selection. If a template is absent, check whether it is active. If an edit cannot save, review permissions and field errors, then reopen the record before retrying. If no assessment appears, check Active/Paused, start date, target membership, active-company status and the resulting Compliance runs; do not create duplicate schedules to compensate for delayed processing.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the Company selector restrict my schedule?">
    No. Schedule configuration is tenant-wide. Choose the intended companies in Targets.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does a new schedule target by default?">
    All active companies in the tenant. Change Targets before saving if you need a narrower assessment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why is my run template missing?">
    The schedule picker offers active templates. Review the template under Settings > Compliance > Runs.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the target summary show the final number of companies?">
    Only a company selection directly counts companies. Group and industry modes count the selected groups or industries.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does pausing require Save Changes?">
    No. The row status switch saves immediately. It does not cancel or delete runs already created.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Last Run mean all checks finished?">
    No. Run creation and check assignment are separate from assessment completion.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I choose an exact time or time zone?">
    The schedule editor offers dates and recurrence patterns, without a time-of-day or time-zone selector.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does deleting a schedule remove completed evidence?">
    No. It removes the schedule for future execution, not the historical assessments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
