Health Score Configuration controls which dimensions can contribute to Client Health. Use it to remove dimensions that do not apply to your MSP's service model.

<Note>
These settings enable or disable dimensions. The current model's base weights, risk bands, evidence rules, and minimum coverage requirements are fixed so scores remain consistent across the portfolio.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

| Permission | Access |
|-----------|--------|
| `read_settings_client_health` | View the scoring configuration |
| `manage_settings_client_health` | Enable or disable dimensions and save changes |

Client Health itself requires `read_customer_success`.

## Open scoring settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click **Settings** in the main sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Scoring">
    Under **MSP Tools**, select **Scoring**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Available dimensions

| Dimension | Base weight | Evidence used |
|-----------|------------:|---------------|
| **Service Quality** | 18% | Eight complete ticket weeks, comparing demand, resolution, backlog size, open-ticket age, and backlog direction |
| **Engagement** | 18% | Meeting recency and a recent NPS response |
| **Financial** | 13% | Profitability, open-invoice health, and payment speed |
| **Sales** | 8% | Opportunity or quote recency and trailing-year decided win rate |
| **Projects** | 10% | Active-project schedule health, recent on-time delivery, and effort adherence |
| **Standards** | 13% | Applicable required standards deployed for the company |
| **Environment** | 10% | Server availability and normalized patch coverage |
| **Operational** | 10% | Planner timeliness, goal progress, and compliance timeliness |

See [Client Health](/user-guides/msp-tools/client-health#how-each-dimension-is-calculated) for the complete calculation and data requirements for every dimension.

## Enable or disable dimensions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the dimensions">
    Each row shows the dimension name, description, and whether it is enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the toggles">
    Disable a dimension only when it does not apply to your service model. Enable it when you want its observed evidence to contribute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the enabled count">
    The summary shows how many of the eight dimensions are enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the configuration">
    Click **Save** in the page header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish new scores">
    Wait for the next daily Client Health refresh at 2:00 AM UTC, or go to **Client Health** and click **Refresh Scores**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How configuration affects scores

When a dimension is disabled:

- It does not contribute to the overall health score or evidence-coverage requirement
- Its base weight is redistributed across the enabled evidence that is available for each company
- Existing observed data can still appear on the company detail page as **Excluded from health score**
- Previously published scores remain unchanged; the configuration takes effect in the next completed scorecard

When a dimension is enabled, MSPortal still requires real evidence. Enabling a dimension does not cause missing data to score zero or receive a healthy default.

<Warning>
Companies need at least three scored dimensions and 40% overall evidence coverage to receive a numeric health score. Disabling several dimensions can make it harder to meet that requirement, even when the remaining dimensions have data.
</Warning>

## When to disable a dimension

Consider disabling a dimension when:

- It is not part of the services you provide to any client
- Its concept is not relevant to how your team evaluates client health
- You have intentionally retired the process or data source across the portfolio

Do not disable a dimension only to hide temporary N/A results. If the dimension is relevant, correct the integration, company mapping, or source data and publish a new scorecard instead.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My changes are not visible in Client Health">
    Scoring settings affect the next completed scorecard. Go to **Client Health** and click **Refresh Scores**, or wait for the daily 2:00 AM UTC refresh.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="More companies show Limited data after I disabled dimensions">
    A numeric score still requires at least three scored dimensions and 40% coverage across the enabled, applicable model. Re-enable relevant dimensions or improve evidence coverage for those that remain.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A disabled dimension still appears on a company">
    If MSPortal has observed data for the dimension, the company detail page can show it for context with the label **Excluded from health score**. It does not affect the health score.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
