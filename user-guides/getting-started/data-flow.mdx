**Audience:** MSP staff planning a client workflow. MSPortal combines synchronized provider information with records your team creates and maintains. The connections below describe possible workflows; they are not a promise that one record automatically creates every downstream record.

## Start with the source and company mapping

Provider integrations bring supported data into MSPortal. Which records arrive depends on the connection, provider permissions, selected scope, synchronization and company mappings. A saved connection or a successful test is not proof that the latest data has arrived.

Native records, such as a goal or Planner item, can also be created directly in MSPortal. They do not require every provider to be connected.

## Move from evidence to a plan

| Work | What to review | Deliberate next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Surveys and assessments | Responses, findings and their date | Create or update the relevant goals or remediation work |
| Devices, cloud and protection data | Source freshness and the mapped company | Investigate the finding and link supporting records where the editor supports it |
| Goals and compliance work | Intended outcome, evidence and ownership | Create or link Planner items for the work you decide to pursue |
| Planner | Scope, dates, status, costs and relationships | Include the intended items in the budget or client discussion |
| Budgets | Included rows, assumptions, overrides and totals | Review the resulting plan and any separate approval workflow |
| Calendar and reporting | Selected company, period, content and filters | Prepare the meeting or report, inspect the result, then share through the intended workflow |

Saving a source record, linking a record and copying values into another record are different operations. For example, a later template edit does not automatically prove that existing applied Planner items changed. Check the relevant guide before expecting an automatic refresh or cascade.

## Keep operational work distinct

PSA tickets and projects can be viewed and reported on without becoming Planner items. Planner supports client roadmapping; it does not turn every operational ticket into planned work. Creating a Planner item or entering an estimate does not by itself issue a quote, approve expenditure or create a PSA project.

## Understand provider actions

Opening a synchronized record is different from an action that creates or updates something in the provider. Treat actions such as creating a ticket or project, changing a provider field or requesting a sync according to that provider’s guide and the action shown.

Do not assume all integrations support the same writeback, that device fields always update the PSA, or that completing an onboarding task always creates a provider project. Check the configured workflow and verify its result before relying on the external record.

## Check freshness before presenting

1. Confirm the correct company and provider instance.
2. Check source timestamps and the latest synchronization result where available.
3. Review record links, report filters and the selected date range.
4. Inspect the rendered report or meeting view. Empty data, a missing mapping and a healthy result are different states.
5. Verify audience visibility before sharing. An MSP preview does not prove the Company audience sees the same result.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does every finding automatically create a Planner item?">
    No. Review the finding and use the supported creation or linking workflow. The data-flow guide describes possible relationships, not an automatic pipeline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Planner estimate create a quote or approve spending?">
    No. Quoting, budget approval and provider project creation are separate workflows with their own actions and permissions.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why can a report show older information than the provider?">
    Check the latest successful synchronization, company mapping, report period and filters. Opening the report does not guarantee a fresh provider request.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every integration write changes back to its source?">
    No. Writeback is provider- and action-specific. Consult the provider guide and verify the external result before relying on it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
