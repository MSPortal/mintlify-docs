**Audience:** MSP staff with permission to edit the relevant record or setting. Company users have their own role and company scope; seeing a record in an MSP preview does not establish what a Company user can access.

## Choose the right control

| Need | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Keep a supported record internal | The record’s **Hide from company users** control, then its Save action |
| Hide a custom field while keeping its parent record available | The custom-field definition’s visibility setting |
| Restrict a dashboard to MSP staff | The dashboard’s visibility setting |
| Decide which modules or actions a Company role can use | Company permissions and role assignment |
| Simplify a sidebar | Navigation preferences; this alone does not revoke access |

Do not assume all record types start with the same visibility. Templates, copied records and existing settings can supply different values. Inspect the actual record before saving or sharing it.

## Make a supported record internal

1. Open the intended Planner item, Goal or Compliance record in its editor.
2. Confirm the company and record identity. Check that you have edit access.
3. Enable **Hide from company users** where the editor offers it.
4. Save the record and reopen it to confirm the setting persisted.
5. Verify the intended Company audience before relying on the change for a rollout. Staff preview and role inspection alone are not a Company-session test.

Changing the checkbox stages the form value; it is not proof of a saved change. Read-only or historical views can disable the control. A hidden parent record, a private note and a hidden custom field are different settings.

## Custom fields and dashboards

Use [Custom Fields](/user-guides/settings/custom-fields) to control a definition’s Company visibility. Hiding a field does not hide its owning company, device or other parent record. Save the definition change through the field editor.

For dashboards, select the intended visibility when creating or editing the dashboard, then save. Shared/private access, internal/client visibility and company-specific dashboard availability are separate choices; review them together in [Dashboard settings](/user-guides/dashboards/manage-dashboard-settings).

## Onboarding visibility

Onboarding editors can use **Visible to client portal**, which has the opposite wording from **Hide from company users**. A template establishes values for launched work; inspect the active portal’s task setting when changing an existing client rollout. Do not assume editing a template retroactively updates every existing portal.

## Before sharing

Check the actual sharing or export workflow as well as the record setting. A visibility change cannot recall a file already downloaded or information already sent outside the portal. If a Company account still sees information you intended to hide, record the affected page and audience for support instead of assuming an MSP-only preview proves the issue resolved.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding a sidebar module make its records private?">
    No. Sidebar preferences change navigation. Use role permissions and the relevant record visibility controls.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the visibility checkbox saved immediately?">
    In the Planner, Goal and Compliance editors, it is part of the form state. Use the record’s Save action and reopen it to verify the result.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does hiding a custom field hide the entire record?">
    No. The field’s visibility is separate from the parent record’s visibility.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does changing a template update every existing onboarding portal?">
    Do not assume that. Inspect and change the active portal’s visibility where needed; the template and active portal are separate editing contexts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does an MSP preview prove the Company audience cannot see a record?">
    No. Check the intended Company role and session. A staff preview has different access and is not a substitute for audience verification.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
