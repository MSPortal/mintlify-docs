**Audience:** MSP administrators with Launch Center management or the relevant tenant-settings access.

## Enter the launch profile

1. Open **Launch Center** and select the company-profile step in **Setup guide**.
2. Enter **Company name**, choose the operating **Timezone**, and review **City** and **Country**.
3. Select **Next** to save through the workspace flow. Correct any highlighted validation error and wait for the result.
4. Revisit the step and check the saved values and readiness finding.

The profile readiness check requires a company name, timezone, and at least a city or country. It does not certify the completeness of your billing address.

## Maintain the full tenant record

For billing contact and other organization preferences, open **Settings > Tenant > Details**. Use the header **Save Settings** for form changes. Branding uploads and other dedicated settings controls have separate save actions; do not assume one header save handles every operation.

Choose the timezone deliberately before scheduling client work. Review billing information in [Billing](/user-guides/settings/billing) as well; profile readiness is not payment verification.

## Troubleshooting

If Next fails, correct the indicated field and retry the save. If another administrator updated the plan, reopen the current values before applying your changes. If editing is unavailable, check both Launch Center and tenant-settings permissions.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a street address for the profile readiness check?">
    The check requires a name, timezone and city or country. Billing address completeness is a separate review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is the old bottom Save Changes button?">
    In Tenant settings, form changes use the header Save Settings. The embedded launch profile saves through Next.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does entering a billing email activate my subscription?">
    No. Review subscription and payment status separately in Billing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
