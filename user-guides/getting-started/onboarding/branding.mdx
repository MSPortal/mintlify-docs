**Audience:** MSP administrators with tenant branding access. Open the branding step in **Launch Center**, or **Settings > Tenant > Branding**.

## Prepare and apply branding

1. Choose the logo variant you want to change. Review the light and dark theme variants and the smaller navigation icons separately.
2. Select **Upload** and choose an image within the displayed format and size limits.
3. For a logo that opens the cropper, position the image and confirm the crop. Wait for the upload result. Merely closing the cropper is not confirmation.
4. Set favicon and reporting-logo assets using their dedicated upload controls. These can upload directly without the logo cropper.
5. Review the brand color control and use its save action. Reopen the page to confirm the persisted result.

Uploads and color saves are separate operations. A later **Save Settings**, Reset or navigation action does not undo an already completed upload.

## Review where the brand appears

Check both light and dark navigation, the compact logo, browser favicon and a report or certificate preview where available. A logo that fits the full sidebar may be unreadable at icon size. Tenant settings also offers sign-in-page customization; review its own controls and save behavior independently.

The launch branding check looks for saved logo evidence and configured colors. It does not check visual quality or require every possible asset. Review the actual rendered experience before rollout.

## Replace or clear assets

Use the appropriate upload control to replace one asset. Review the scope of any clear-all action before using it; clearing branding affects the organization, not just your own theme. If an upload fails, inspect the saved preview before retrying. If a favicon appears stale, reload or revisit the page before uploading duplicate files.

## FAQs

  <Accordion title="Does closing the cropper save the logo?">
    No. Confirm the crop and wait for the upload result.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Reset undo a completed branding upload?">
    Do not rely on it. Uploads and color saves are separate persisted operations.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a passing branding check prove every logo looks correct?">
    No. Review light, dark, compact and reporting placements visually.
  </Accordion>
