**Audience:** MSP staff with Billing settings access. Open the billing step in **Launch Center**, or **Settings > Billing**.

## Review the account

1. Check the displayed subscription or trial status and any billing-service error.
2. Review the current plan, billing currency, usage and upcoming invoice information offered for the account. Metered and licensed subscriptions can show different measures.
3. Use the billing-management control to open the hosted billing portal when you need to manage payment or subscription details.
4. Review and confirm any change in that portal. Returning to MSPortal is not itself confirmation that a payment-method or subscription change succeeded.
5. Reopen or refresh Billing and check the resulting status. Review the organization's billing email in Tenant settings as well.

Use the actual account's displayed plan and terms. There is no universal Starter/Professional/Enterprise company-limit table in this setup workflow. Do not infer a price, trial duration or cancellation effect from a different tenant's screenshot.

## Save boundaries

Billing management opens a separate hosted workflow. There is no single local Save button that confirms all payment and subscription actions. Launch progress is also separate from a billing-provider transaction; completing a setup step does not prove a payment succeeded.

## Troubleshooting

If billing data fails to load, inspect the displayed error and retry the reader before repeating any purchase or payment action. If the hosted portal does not open, check the result and browser navigation behavior. If a change is unclear, inspect the current subscription and invoices rather than submitting it again.
