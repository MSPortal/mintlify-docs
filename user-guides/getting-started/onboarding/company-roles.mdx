**Audience:** MSP staff with Company-user settings access. Open **Settings > Users & Roles > Team Roles**, or the account-team step in Launch Center.

Team Roles describe responsibilities such as account manager or technician. They are not merely job-title labels for client users: staff assignments can constrain their company selector and determine who appears in the client's team menu. Access Groups separately control permissions.

## Define and assign responsibilities

1. Review existing Team Roles. Add or edit the role name and description when required, then save the row.
2. Open the company's team-assignment controls and choose the appropriate staff members for each responsibility.
3. Check the selected company before applying assignments. Bulk assignment controls can apply changes immediately to multiple selected companies.
4. Review the client team-menu configuration if you want selected roles and their assigned members to appear in the portal. Check displayed contact details before rollout.

A role definition alone does not assign anyone to a company. Launch readiness checks that every rollout company has an assignment using a defined Team Role.

## Provider-managed teams

Where PSA role mappings are offered, review which responsibilities are synchronized. For mapped ConnectWise roles, synchronization can add and remove members according to the PSA; unmapped responsibilities remain manually managed. Check the provider mapping before correcting an assignment that keeps changing.

## Troubleshooting

If a company still lacks team evidence, check for an actual assignment rather than only a named role. If an internal user's company selector changes, review their team assignments and access group. If the client cannot see an expected team member, check the selected company, role assignment and team-menu visibility.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Are Team Roles just client-user job titles?">
    No. They assign responsibilities, can affect staff company scope and control the client team display.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does adding a role complete account-team setup?">
    No. Each rollout company needs an actual user assignment to a defined role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does a mapped ConnectWise team assignment change?">
    Mapped ConnectWise roles synchronize membership from the PSA, including removals.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
