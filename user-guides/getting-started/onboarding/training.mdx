**Audience:** MSP staff with Training settings access. Open the training step in **Launch Center**, or **Settings > Training**.

## Prepare the training audience

1. Review the available course catalog and any provider or subscription prerequisites. Do not assume every tenant has identical content.
2. Enable the intended courses for the rollout companies, using global or company-specific availability as appropriate.
3. Prepare a learning path containing accessible courses and assign it to the intended users or groups.
4. Save through each screen's offered controls and verify the resulting course availability and assignments.
5. Review the actual Company learner's training view, then return to Launch Center to inspect the findings.

A course appearing in the catalog is not the same as being enabled for a company. A saved learning-path definition is not the same as an assignment to a learner.

## Understand readiness

Course readiness requires enabled course evidence for every rollout company. Assignment readiness looks for learning-path assignments to active users or groups with an accessible course. Merely opening Training settings or marking a step complete does not create either kind of evidence.

These checks do not prove that anyone watched a lesson, passed an assessment or received a reminder. Completion reporting and communications are separate workflows. Review notification settings before actions that could notify learners.

## Troubleshooting

If a company has no course evidence, check global/company availability and provider access. If the assignment finding fails, check that the path is assigned to an eligible active audience and contains a course they can access. If a learner cannot see a course, review both training permissions and effective course availability before creating a duplicate path.

## FAQs

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does a course in the catalog mean clients can take it?">
    No. Confirm effective course availability for the Company audience.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does creating a learning path assign it?">
    No. Save assignments to the intended eligible users or groups separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does training readiness prove course completion?">
    No. It checks configuration and assignment evidence, not learner completion or message delivery.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
